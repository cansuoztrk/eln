**Description:**

Start with a single simit cart on the Karaköy pier and build the tastiest street-food empire Istanbul has ever seen! 🥯☕🐱

Tap to sell, open new stands, and put an adorable cat manager in charge of each one, so business keeps booming even while you’re away.

**FEATURES**
• **10 iconic street foods:** simit, Turkish tea, poğaça, fish sandwiches, döner, kumpir, stuffed mussels, Maraş ice cream, Turkish delight and kebab
• **10 adorable cat managers:** snow-white Pamuk, Boncuk with her evil-eye bead, Paşa the gentleman in a fez, Sultan the odd-eyed Van cat…
• **8 legendary districts:** Karaköy, Kadıköy, Balat, Sultanahmet, Beşiktaş, Galata, Ortaköy and the Princes’ Islands, each with its own skyline and time of day
• **Catch seagulls:** tap the simit thief and it drops a bag of coins
• **Meet the ferry:** when it docks, rush hour begins: income ×3!
• **Earn while you’re away:** your cats keep the shop running
• **Secret recipes & neighbourhood festivals:** triple your profits; level every stand together for ×2 on everything
• **Tulips & district badges:** move to a new district and grow faster with permanent multipliers
• **Daily rewards, quests and achievements**
• **Play offline.** No ads that interrupt you, only optional rewarded videos

Relaxing music, cozy cats and the flavour of Istanbul: Simit Tycoon is the idle tycoon game you’ll want to check on every day.
