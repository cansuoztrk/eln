Klasik solitaire’in dinlendiren kart dizme keyfi, kelime çağrışımıyla buluştu! 📚🐈

• **Günün Rafı:** Herkes için aynı bulmaca. Serini koru, sonucunu arkadaşlarınla paylaş.
• **Nadir kitap koleksiyonu:** Her semt Tekir’in antika rafına yeni bir hazine ekler.
• **Günlük görevler, sandık ve 7 günlük hediye takvimi.**

Kelime oyunlarını, solitaire’i ve kedileri seviyorsan, Tekir seni sahafında bekliyor. Bir çay koy, rafları düzenlemeye başla! ☕

## 🇬🇧 English — App Store

**Name (23):** Bookcat: Word Solitaire

**Subtitle (25):** Cozy Category Card Puzzle

**Keywords (100):** `associations,connections,brain,trivia,crossword,scrabble,klondike,spider,relaxing,library,books,sort`

**Promotional text (155):** Sort words into their shelves and tidy up Tekir’s bookshop! Every level is solvable, no ads mid-level, and a new Daily Shelf arrives every day. Grab a tea!

**Description:**

The relaxing card-sorting joy of classic solitaire meets clever word associations! 📚🐈

The books in Tekir’s cozy Istanbul bookshop are all mixed up. Place a shelf label on an empty shelf, then find the words that belong there: Apple, Cherry, Watermelon → the **Fruits** shelf! Stack words of the same category in your columns and move them together to save moves.

**WHY BOOKCAT?**
• **Every level is solvable:** levels are generated by actually solving them, so every one can be won without ads or purchases.
• **Fair ads:** never in the middle of a level. The first 12 levels are completely ad-free.
• **No lives, no waiting:** lost? Try again right away. Undo is free.
• **129 categories in 4 languages:** from fruits to dinosaurs, from cat breeds to classic novels and Greek gods.
• **An Istanbul tour:** 30 districts, 300+ handcrafted-feeling levels and endless play beyond.
• **Daily Shelf:** the same puzzle for everyone. Keep your streak and share your result.
• **Rare-book collection:** every district adds a treasure to Tekir’s antique shelf.
• **Daily quests, a reward chest and a 7-day gift calendar.**
• **Hints and the Paw:** stuck? Tekir’s paw fetches exactly the book you need.
• **Easy on the eyes:** big, readable cards and a Large Text option.
• **Play offline.**

If you love word games, solitaire and cats, Tekir is waiting for you in the bookshop. Put the kettle on and start shelving! ☕

*Free to play with optional in-app purchases and ads.*
