## 1. Summary
Simit Tycoon does **not** require an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your game progress (stands, districts, gold, settings) is stored **on your device**. To keep the game free, we show ads, and our advertising partners process certain device data for that purpose.

## 2. Data we process
| Data | Source | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID (IDFA/GAID), coarse location derived from IP, device model, OS | Google AdMob and mediation partners | Serving and measuring ads, fraud prevention | Consent (ATT on iOS; consent form in the EEA/UK) / legitimate interest |
| Purchase records, anonymous app user ID | Apple App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat | Delivering and restoring purchases, preventing fraud | Performance of a contract |
| In-game events and crash reports (if enabled) | The app | Improving the game, fixing bugs | Legitimate interest |
| Notification preferences | Device | “Till is full” and daily-reward reminders (only if you allow them) | Consent |

**We never see your payment card details** — payments are processed by Apple or Google.

## 3. Sharing
We share data with service providers: **Google LLC (AdMob)**, **RevenueCat Inc.**, **Apple Inc.** and **Google Play**. Some are located outside your country (e.g. the USA); transfers rely on your consent or on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. **We do not sell your personal information** (CCPA/CPRA).

## 4. Retention
On-device game data is kept until you delete the app or use *Settings › Reset progress*. Data held by providers is retained according to their own policies.

## 5. Your rights
You may access, correct, delete, object to processing, and withdraw consent. To limit ad tracking:
- **iOS:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › turn off Simit Tycoon.
- **Android:** Settings › Privacy › Ads › reset or delete advertising ID.
- **In game:** Settings › Ad privacy choices (EEA/UK).

Email **support@tekirstudios.com** — we reply within 30 days.

## 6. Children
Simit Tycoon is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy and will announce material changes in the game.
