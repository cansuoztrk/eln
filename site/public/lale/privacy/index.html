Lale: Exam Countdown & Focus: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. In short

Lale has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your focus sessions, questions and mock results, garden, friend codes and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

2. Data that stays on your device

  • Exam profile (exam, track, exam date) and daily goals: for the countdown, subject list and scoring.
  • Focus sessions, practice questions, mock results, tulip collection, streak: for statistics and the garden.
  • Optional nickname and the friend codes you add: for the weekly leaderboard.
  • Notification and display preferences.

Study code: when you tap “Share my code”, Lale creates a short text with your nickname, exam type and that week’s totals (focus time, questions, streak, tulips). You share it, for example by message. It is never sent to us and contains nothing else.

Backup: an exported backup file stays under your control.

3. Third parties

  • Apple App Store / Google Play process purchases. Your payment details stay with the store.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (USA) delivers and restores Lale Gardener purchases, using a random anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt.

If you never buy anything, the app shares no personal data with any server. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

4. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off in your phone settings at any time.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete the app or use Settings › Delete all data. RevenueCat keeps anonymous purchase records for as long as store and tax rules require.

6. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and object. You can delete on-device data yourself. For purchase records, email destek@tekirstudios.com; we reply within 30 days.

7. Children and teens

Lale is designed for students aged 13+ and adult exam candidates. With no accounts, ads or tracking, it does not collect personal data.

8. Independence

Lale is not affiliated with ÖSYM, MEB, YÖK or any exam authority. Exam dates are for information only.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will announce significant changes in the app.
